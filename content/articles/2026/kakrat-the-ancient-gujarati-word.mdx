## A note on the word

Kakrat is used in Gujarati in the sense of loud commotion or discordant noise. Gujarati literary usage also connects the word directly with the clamour of crows; for example, the expression "કાગડાં કાકરાટ" appears in Gujarati writer Pinky Dalal's નષ્ટો મોહઃ.

The interpretation developed in this essay — "noise with ego" — is our playful philosophical extension of the word, rather than a formal dictionary definition.

Source: [Pinky Dalal, નષ્ટો મોહઃ, Gujarati literary text, Matrubharti](https://gujarati.matrubharti.com/book/read/content/19952611/lost-fascination-by-pinki-dalal)
