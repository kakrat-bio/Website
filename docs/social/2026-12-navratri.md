text:
At a big community event, everyone assumes someone else has checked on the children. Usually that's true. One Navratri night in Jamnagar, it wasn't.

In her first essay for Kakrat, creative nonfiction writer Foram Trivedi remembers being eight years old, the last girl waiting after the garba with her friend at one in the morning. The organisers had gone home, each thinking another had checked. A stranger offered to take them home, and they refused, because their mothers had taught them never to go with one. Two neighbours found them and led them back to their mothers, who were still dancing.

It's a gentle story with a clear lesson for anyone who runs events, schools or teams: when everyone is responsible, make sure someone actually is.
