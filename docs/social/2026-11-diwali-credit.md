text:
Kakrat's first guest essay is up.

Sonia Kashyap, a law student and freelance writer in Delhi, took one Buy Now, Pay Later product and read every disclosure behind it. The app you tap is not the lender. A ₹10,000 purchase turned into a three-month EMI costs ₹706 to ₹959 more. On the 15-day cycle, a late payment costs up to ₹50 a day for about two months. And under the RBI's digital lending rules, all of it can reach your credit report.

Her advice is simple: before you tap, find out who is lending, what it costs in rupees, when it's due and what happens if you're late. It's all in the Key Fact Statement.
